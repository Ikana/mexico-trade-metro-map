Glosario / Glossary
TEU
Twenty-foot Equivalent Unit — standard measure of container volume
Maquiladora
Export-oriented factory operating under special customs rules near the US-Mexico border
Intermodal
Freight transport using multiple modes (truck + rail) with standardized containers
Breakbulk
Non-containerized cargo loaded individually (vehicles, machinery, steel)
Nearshoring
Relocating manufacturing to a nearby country (e.g., from China to Mexico)
USMCA
United States–Mexico–Canada Agreement (replaced NAFTA in 2020)
