# 🚇 Mexico Trade Metro Map

A subway/metro-style schematic map of Mexico's land trade corridors — think London Tube map, but for freight.

Lines represent major trade routes, stations represent cities, ports, and border crossings, and station size reflects trade volume. The map makes Mexico's trade infrastructure instantly legible to anyone: logistics operators, investors, policymakers.

## Why

- **Nearshoring is exploding**: Mexico surpassed China as #1 US trade partner in 2023
- **No one has made this visualization**: existing maps are either geographic (hard to read) or text-based (boring)
- **US-Mexico freight hit $872.8B in 2025** — 73.6% by truck, 10.9% by rail

## Inspiration

- [Wallenius Wilhelmsen Ocean Trade Maps](https://www.walleniuswilhelmsen.com/what-we-do/ocean-transportation/trade-maps) by Cameron Booth — subway-style shipping route maps
- Harry Beck's London Underground map (1931) — the original schematic transit map
